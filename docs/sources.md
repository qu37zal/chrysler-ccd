Additional lead: Workshop Manuals service article says the 1999 Prowler CCD is a twisted pair, each line roughly 2.5 V, with a voltage divider network. Snap-on communications manual identifies Chrysler CCD at DLC pins 3 and 11; exact Prowler manual diagram must confirm. Chrysler documentation for related model years shows SCI lines on other DLC pins; record Prowler-specific assignments before designing an all-port monitor. PCI uses DLC pin 2 on applicable vehicles and is not CCD.
